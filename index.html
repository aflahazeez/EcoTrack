Verified Environmental Benchmarks

EcoTrack grounds your individual calculations in published environmental research and institutional databases. Standard reference benchmarks include:

1.9 t CO₂e
India Per-Capita / Year

The average annual greenhouse gas emission per person in India, serving as an everyday baseline for evaluating individual lifestyle footprints.

Source: Global Carbon Project & Our World in Data (2022–2023)
4.5 t CO₂e
Global Per-Capita / Year

The worldwide average annual greenhouse gas emission per person across both industrialized and developing economies.

Source: International Energy Agency (IEA) & World Bank
0.82 kg / kWh
Indian Grid Intensity

Estimated end-consumer emissions per unit of grid electricity in India, accounting for generation mix and ~18% transmission losses.

Source: Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Baseline Ver. 18 (2023)
21.8 kg / yr
Mature Tree Sequestration

The estimated annual carbon dioxide absorbed by a healthy mature tree, used to calculate tree-equivalent absorption metrics.

Source: US EPA GHG Equivalencies & Arbor Day Foundation (~48 lbs/yr)
