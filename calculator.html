Scientific Accounting Boundaries & Scope Disclosure

Included Operational Boundaries:
  • Transportation: Passenger travel in kg CO₂e. Tailpipe Scope 1 direct combustion (fuel) and Scope 2 operational charging/traction (EV/metro at busbar).
  • Grid Electricity: 0.675 kg CO₂e/kWh net generation at busbar (CEA Database Ver. 22.0, August 2026, for FY 2025–26). Excludes T&D losses.
  • Cooking Gas: 2.993 kg CO₂e/kg LPG (IPCC Tier 1 with AR5 GWPs: 2.985 kg CO₂ + 0.008 kg non-CO₂ CH₄ and N₂O).
  • Household Waste: Landfill anaerobic methane decay model (Scope 3 provisional heuristic).
Excluded Lifecycle Boundaries:
  • Diet & food agriculture (removed per specification).
  • Commercial air travel & freight shipping.
  • Manufactured consumer goods & electronic embodied carbon.
  • Public municipal infrastructure & commercial heating.

Consistent CO₂e Accounting: Every category is expressed in kilograms of carbon dioxide equivalent (kg CO₂e), enabling internally consistent category comparisons, percentage share calculations, and total aggregation.

Note on National Baselines: Comparisons against national all-sector per-capita averages (e.g. 1.9 t CO₂e) are deliberately omitted because national baselines include heavy industry, mining, power sector, freight, and agriculture; comparing household operational sub-scopes against all-sector totals is scientifically flawed.
