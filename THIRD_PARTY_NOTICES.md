## 4. Environmental Data Sources & Accounting Disclosures

EcoTrack distinguishes verified institutional primary benchmarks from EcoTrack-derived engineering assumptions and heuristic lifestyle models. No institution listed below endorses or sponsors EcoTrack.

### Central Electricity Authority (CEA) of India

- **Reference Document:** *CO₂ Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector, User Guide, Version 22.0 (August 2026)*
- **Publishing Authority:** Central Electricity Authority, Ministry of Power, Government of India, New Delhi
- **Source URL:** [https://cea.nic.in/cdm-co2-baseline-view?lang=en&mappingId=234](https://cea.nic.in/cdm-co2-baseline-view?lang=en&mappingId=234)
- **Reference Table:** Table 1 (*Weighted Average Emission Rate, Simple Operating Margin, Build Margin and Combined Margin of the National Grid*)
- **Reported Value:** **$0.675\text{ t CO}_2\text{/MWh}$** ($= 0.675\text{ kg CO}_2\text{/kWh}$) for Fiscal Year 2025–26 (national weighted-average emission rate including renewable energy sources).
- **Accounting Boundary & Disclosures:**
  1. **Direct Generation / Busbar Boundary:** The CEA factor measures net electricity generation stack $\text{CO}_2$ at power station busbars.
  2. **Pure $\text{CO}_2$, Not $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$:** The CEA benchmark reports exclusively $\text{CO}_2$ emissions. It does not measure or calculate non-$\text{CO}_2$ gases ($\text{CH}_4$, $\text{N}_2\text{O}$).
  3. **EcoTrack Aggregate Use:** EcoTrack uses this $0.675\text{ kg CO}_2\text{/kWh}$ figure as an operational approximation in its aggregate greenhouse gas total ($\text{kg CO}_2\text{e}$) to allow unified comparison with fuel combustion and waste.
  4. **Exclusion of Losses & Upstream:** Transmission and distribution (T&D) losses (typically 15–20% in Indian regional grids) are **not included**. Upstream coal mining, rail transport, and plant construction are excluded.

### IPCC Guidelines (Domestic Cooking Gas - LPG)

- **Reference Document:** *2006 IPCC Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories*, Volume 2 (*Energy*), Chapter 2 (*Stationary Combustion*), Table 2.2 and Table 2.5; and IPCC Fifth Assessment Report (AR5) 100-year Global Warming Potentials.
- **Publishing Authority:** Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (WMO / UNEP)
- **Source URL:** [https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/vol2.html](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/vol2.html)
- **Implemented Calculation Basis:**
  - Default net calorific value (NCV): $0.04731\text{ GJ/kg LPG}$
  - Default carbon emission factor: $63.1\text{ kg CO}_2\text{/GJ}$
  - Direct stoichiometric $\text{CO}_2$: $63.1 \times 0.04731 = 2.98526\text{ kg CO}_2\text{/kg LPG}$
  - Incomplete combustion $\text{CH}_4$: $0.005\text{ kg/GJ} \times 0.04731 \times 28\text{ (AR5 GWP)} = 0.00662\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$
  - Non-$\text{CO}_2$ $\text{N}_2\text{O}$: $0.0001\text{ kg/GJ} \times 0.04731 \times 265\text{ (AR5 GWP)} = 0.00125\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$
  - Combined Scope 1 combustion factor: $2.98526 + 0.00662 + 0.00125 = \mathbf{2.993\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e / kg LPG}}$
- **Cylinder Capacities Configured:**
  - $14.2\text{ kg}$ standard domestic cylinder: $14.2 \times 2.99314 = \mathbf{42.50\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}}$
  - $5.0\text{ kg}$ mini domestic cylinder: $5.0 \times 2.99314 = \mathbf{14.97\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}}$
  - $19.0\text{ kg}$ commercial cylinder: $19.0 \times 2.99314 = \mathbf{56.87\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}}$
- **Accounting Boundary:** Direct residential Scope 1 complete combustion only. Upstream petroleum refining and distribution are excluded.

### EcoTrack-Derived Transport Assumptions

The following transport factors are **EcoTrack-derived engineering calculations**, synthesized from published fleet averages, certified model ratings, and test cycles. Neither ARAI, BEE, DMRC, Indian Railways, nor CIRT publishes or endorses these final emission factors.

| Transport Mode | Implemented Factor | Unit | Calculation Basis & Data Synthesis | Known Limitations & Uncertainties |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Provisional Waste Heuristic Assumptions

- **Configured Values:**
  - Unsegregated municipal solid waste to open landfill: **`0.45 kg CO₂e / kg`**
  - Recyclables separated (dry collection): **`0.20 kg CO₂e / kg`**
  - Organic composting (aerobic): **`0.08 kg CO₂e / kg`**
- **Explicit Institutional Disclaimer:**
  **The Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) of India does NOT publish, calculate, or endorse these three numerical emission factors.**
- **Nature of the Factors:**
  These values are **provisional EcoTrack heuristic scaling assumptions** chosen to illustrate the relative qualitative benefits of household waste segregation and home composting in a lifestyle tool.
- **Contextual References Used:**
  - **CPCB Solid Waste Management Reports:** Consulted solely for general contextual information regarding Indian municipal solid waste characterization (notably that Indian MSW typically contains approximately 45% to 55% compostable biodegradable organic matter).
  - **IPCC 2006 Guidelines, Volume 5 (*Waste*):** Consulted for qualitative principles regarding anaerobic landfill methane generation and aerobic composting. The three numerical values ($0.45$, $0.20$, and $0.08\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$) are **not** mathematically derived from or published by IPCC Volume 5 equations.

## 5. Summary of Methodological Uncertainties

To maintain scientific integrity, the following uncertainties in the project's data architecture are formally acknowledged:

1. **Delivered vs. Busbar Electricity:** Household electricity consumption is measured at the consumer meter, whereas the CEA benchmark ($0.675\text{ kg CO}_2\text{/kWh}$) is measured at the power station busbar. National transmission and distribution losses (~15–20%) are omitted to avoid regional speculation, resulting in a conservative footprint estimate.
2. **CO₂ vs. CO₂e Equivalence in Electricity:** Electricity emissions account exclusively for $\text{CO}_2$ from thermal power stations and omit non-$\text{CO}_2$ gases.
3. **Vehicle Fleet Representativeness:** EV and two-wheeler ratings rely on representative EcoTrack assumptions rather than telematics-averaged operational data.
4. **Waste Degradation Variabilities:** Real-world landfill methane emissions vary widely with moisture content, compaction depth, and regional temperature. The heuristic factors ($0.45$, $0.20$, and $0.08\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$) provide illustrative scaling rather than direct measurement.
